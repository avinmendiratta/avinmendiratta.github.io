# When are we…

Primary: Ivan

Secondary: Ādi

<span style="color: purple;"><em>When are we, where are we and how did we get here?</em></span>

I have 2 more problems for you - ‘who’ as in “who are you?” and ‘why’ as in “why are you asking me this question?”. Having 3 of such problems already troubling you, you needn’t resolve the two’s posed by me - for now!

I am going to give you an answer. I don't know whether you will understand it or not. The fact that you ask this question leads me to believe that you won’t, atleast not for now, and so I think the ‘how’ becomes extremely important for you.

Today is Chaitra Shukla Pratipada, 2140 i.e., the New Year. ~~That’s it for the when part.~~ Someone as lost as you probably shouldn’t be concerned by time, ah but perhaps you are looking for a bite of historical context. Let’s talk about that later.

The land that you stand on is called **Airāvat**. Again, a finer microscope on the geography for later.

Now to the more pertinent question! Right now, I don't know how you got here. And, frankly, I don’t understand the context of the ‘how’. Well, whatever your means of conveyance may have been, you are weary for it. Your body needs rest, but your mind continues to search and fret as if it has had been perpetually curious. Anyways, the town onwards has an eatery with many scrumptious delights neatly prepared. Allow me to take you there.

<span style="color: purple;"><em>The question of my sustenance troubles me!</em></span>

Let me take care of your continual sustenance, iff. you allow me to accompany you on your journey.

<span style="color: purple;"><em>Let me be of more assistance!</em></span>

I think I could require your assistance, as a companion.

<span style="color: purple;"><em>Who are You?</em></span>

Who am I? You can call me Ivan. What may I call, you?

<span style="color: purple;"><em>…</em></span>

How do you prefer Ādi, it means ‘beginning’?

<span style="color: purple;"><em>Thank You</em></span>

Please, I am as much your helper as you are mine.
Henceforth, I am your friend!
Let us talk more on the way!
